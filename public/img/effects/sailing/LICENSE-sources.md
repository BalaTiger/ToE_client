# Sailing particle sources

Verified and downloaded on 2026-09-18. Only the two unmodified PNG files below
were extracted into this directory; the complete asset pack and Unity package
are not shipped with the game.

## Kenney Particle Pack

- Creator: Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl).
- Original author / license page: <https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack>.
- Official download: <https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/particle-pack/f8fe0f8cb8-1677578741/kenney_particle-pack.zip>.
- License: Creative Commons Zero / CC0 1.0 Universal, public-domain dedication.
- License terms: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
- The official page identifies the pack as CC0. The archive's original
  `License.txt` additionally permits personal and commercial projects and states
  that attribution is welcome but not mandatory. A byte-for-byte copy is kept
  here as `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`, including the pack's additional template credits.
- Pack identification: the official web page calls the release 1.0 (2018), while
  its current archive's license identifies Particle Pack (1.1).

| Local file | Original archive path | Dimensions | Bytes | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `kenney-foam-particles.png` | `PNG (Transparent)/dirt_01.png` | 512 × 512 RGBA | 47,313 | Small scattered foam/spray accents, scaled down and tinted at render time |
| `kenney-droplet-soft.png` | `PNG (Transparent)/circle_05.png` | 512 × 512 RGBA | 65,336 | Tiny soft droplets or defocused highlights |

The local names describe their use in this game. They are general-purpose
particle masks, not photographs or a dedicated water simulation. They supplement
the separately authored main wave artwork. Their PNG bytes, color channels,
dimensions and alpha channels have not been modified; only filenames changed.

No code, video, account access, purchase, or third-party runtime is required.
